## **About The Mod**

### Who said a Western mod needs guns?

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1908/543/ezgif-5d244003121f5428-gif.gif)

Adventure into the world of prospecting at the peak of a gold rush with a dream to get rich.

* Find new sandstone and red sandstone variants of coal, copper, iron and gold in deserts and mesas.

* Discover the new ores: silver, a valuable mineral used for making hats and pocket watches, and pyrite, also known as fool's gold.

* Introduce villagers to prospecting by providing them with a furnace as a job block and trade for high prices.

* Craft a gold pan and sift river water in the hope of finding valuable resources to trade with the prospector villager.

*   Connect Dynamite and a Detonator using wire to blow up walls, caves and more in a safer, controlled manner.
*   Craft three different styles of cowboy hats and dye them to suit your style
*   Paint your wooden planks to create realistic, colourful western towns - and use an axe to remove the colour whenever you want

Golden Frontier was made for the 2026 Curseforge ModJam
